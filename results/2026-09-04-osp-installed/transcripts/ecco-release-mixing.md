## The premise needs one correction before anything else

"Whatever ECCO SSH collections cover the full period" is the trap in this task, and it's worth stating plainly: **you do not need more than one collection, and combining two would corrupt the trend you're asking about.**

PO.DAAC ships a corrected `V4R4B` re-release of SSH alongside the original `V4R4`. Per `knowledge/podaac/gotchas/ecco-release-mixing.md` (stable, severity high, human-verified 2026-09-04), an SSH series assembled from a mix of the two "folds the release correction into the geophysical signal: an apparent step or drift at the release boundary is an artifact of the switch, not the ocean." Nothing errors - the ShortNames differ by one character.

I checked CMR (metadata only, no downloads). Both monthly llc90 SSH collections already span the whole period:

| ShortName | concept_id | coverage | granules 1992-01->2017-12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ECCO_L4_SSH_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4` | C1991543813-POCLOUD | 1992-01-01 -> 2018-01-01 | 312 |
| `ECCO_L4_SSH_LLC0090GRID_MONTHLY_V4R4B` | C2129189870-POCLOUD | 1992-01-01 -> 2018-01-01 | 312 |

312 = 26  x  12, complete years, no gaps at either end. **Use `..._V4R4B` alone** (DOI 10.5067/ECL5M-SSH4B) - the gotcha's correct approach is "one release throughout and states it," with V4R4B being the corrected release for the SSH and OBP families.

## The rest of the specification

- **Variant `SSH`** (IB-corrected), named and never mixed with `SSHNOIBC`, `SSHIBC` or `ETAN` - `gotchas/ecco-ssh-ib-variants.md` (stable) and `fields/ecco-v4r4/ssh.md` (stable, CMR sweep + human-verified).
- **Native llc90, not `05DEG`** - area-weighted basin means want the native `rA` cell areas; `gotchas/ecco-native-vs-regridded.md` (stable, high). The draft `validity-domains/ecco-large-scale-statistics.md` supports mean/trend/variability claims on native monthly fields over exactly 1992-2017; being draft, it is unverified, and its `releases:` field still lists only V4R4, so it hasn't been extended to V4R4B on paper.
- **Trend method**: fit the slope and the twelve monthly offsets **jointly**, then put the interval on the effective sample size. Deseasonalizing first and fitting second hands the climatology 143/(144Y^2-1) of the trend (`gotchas/ecco-trend-deseasonalize-jointly.md`, stable/high); a slope with a textbook error bar over-claims by roughly sqrt((1+r1)/(1-r1)) (`gotchas/ecco-trend-without-effective-n.md`, stable/high - calibration measures 47-49% coverage for the naive interval at r1 0.8 against 92-95% for the corrected one).
